# Evidence-Oriented Scoring

The opportunity MVP prioritizes observable indicators over pseudo-precise demand scores. Provider counts, nearest identified-provider distance, geographic scope, dataset dates, and source limitations remain attached to each evidence item.

Distance uses explicit 0-2 km, 2-5 km, and 5-10 km bands. Straight-line distance is labeled `straight_line_haversine`; it is not presented as road access.

Missing required evidence blocks opportunity display. Population, agricultural activity, livestock counts, and mapped businesses may be indicators or proxies, but are not direct measurements of demand.
